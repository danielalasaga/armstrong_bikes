# Incoterm Cost Allocation Rules

## FOB (Free on Board)
- **Cost paid by supplier:** Factory cost + packaging + internal transport to port + port loading
- **Cost paid by buyer:** Ocean freight, insurance, port discharge, inland transport, customs clearance, import duty, tariff
- **Risk transfer:** At vessel rail (port side) — supplier liable until goods cross ship rail
- **Landed cost formula:** Unit price + ocean freight + insurance (0.5% of total) + duty

## CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)
- **Cost paid by supplier:** Factory + packaging + internal transport + port loading + ocean freight + insurance
- **Cost paid by buyer:** Port discharge, inland transport, customs clearance, import duty, tariff
- **Risk transfer:** Same as FOB (vessel rail) but supplier insures
- **Landed cost formula:** Unit price + duty only (freight and insurance are in unit price)

## DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)
- **Cost paid by supplier:** Everything including destination delivery and customs clearance
- **Cost paid by buyer:** Nothing except the invoice total
- **Risk transfer:** At buyer's facility (maximum supplier liability)
- **Landed cost formula:** Unit price only (all-in)

## EXW (Ex Works)
- **Cost paid by supplier:** Factory cost + packaging only
- **Cost paid by buyer:** Everything from factory gate onward
- **Risk transfer:** At supplier's facility door
- **Landed cost formula:** Unit price + all freight + all duty

## FCA (Free Carrier)
- **Cost paid by supplier:** Factory + packaging + delivery to carrier (could be port, airport, or warehouse)
- **Cost paid by buyer:** Freight from that point onward + insurance + duty
- **Risk transfer:** At carrier handoff point
- **Landed cost formula:** Unit price + ocean freight from carrier point + insurance + duty
