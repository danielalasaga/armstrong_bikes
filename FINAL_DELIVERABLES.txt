1. BOM STRUCTURE (11 KB, ~350 lines)
   File: bom-structure.md
   Purpose: Teaches agent about bike models, parts, criticality, constraints
   Contains: Three bike models (Commuter, Mountain, Road), 30+ parts, 
             critical path definition, substitutability rules, MOQ implications
   Used by: Coordinator (to understand what's a bike and what matters)

2. COST OPTIMIZER (9.9 KB, ~280 lines)
   File: cost-optimizer.md
   Purpose: Calculates landed cost and finds cheapest sourcing plan
   Contains: Landed cost formula, incoterm handling (FOB/CIF/DDP), 
             duty/freight/insurance/MOQ calculations, worked examples, 
             BOM aggregation, cost optimization strategies
   Used by: Coordinator (specialist 1: find lowest-cost suppliers)

3. LEAD-TIME CRITICAL PATH (11 KB, ~330 lines)
   File: lead-time-critical-path.md
   Purpose: Identifies bottlenecks and checks deadline compliance
   Contains: Critical path algorithm, assembly start date calculation, 
             late scenario mitigation (express freight, faster suppliers),
             buffer strategy, timeline analysis
   Used by: Coordinator (specialist 2: confirm we're on-time)

4. SOURCING RECOMMENDATION (15 KB, ~420 lines)
   File: sourcing-recommendation.md
   Purpose: Formats final decision as professional sourcing memo
   Contains: Memo structure (header, executive summary, cost analysis, 
             lead-time analysis, decision rationale, scenarios), output JSON format
   Used by: Coordinator (specialist 3: format output)

WHAT YOU NEED TO BUILD (The Coordinator Agent)
───────────────────────────────────────────────

This is your 90-minute task:

1. Coordinator Logic (the decision tree)
   - Load bom-structure.md
   - Call Cost Specialist (claude with cost-optimizer.md context)
   - Call Lead-Time Specialist (claude with lead-time-critical-path.md context)
   - DECISION: If lead-time says "late", ask cost specialist for alternatives
   - Call Output Formatter (claude with sourcing-recommendation.md context)

2. Synthetic Data (supplier quotes, bike orders, ground truth)
   - Suppliers: 12–15 suppliers with prices by part, lead times, MOQs
   - Scenarios: 12–15 bike sourcing orders (model, volume, deadline, suppliers)
   - Ground truth: cheapest plan cost, bottleneck part, on-time? yes/no

3. Evaluation Loop
   - Run coordinator on 12–15 scenarios
   - Check: did it pick the right suppliers?
   - Log: accuracy %, tokens per specialist, cost per decision

4. Demo
   - Show one live run (40 seconds)
   - Explain the decision (60 seconds)
   - Show output memo (30 seconds)
   - Close on the value (20 seconds)

THE PITCH
─────────

"A bike has 35 parts from 12 suppliers. The cheapest sourcing plan might be
late. The fastest plan might be too expensive. A sourcing analyst would spend
a day normalizing quotes and running scenarios. Our system does it in 90
seconds: cost specialist finds cheapest, lead-time specialist checks if it's
on-time, coordinator resolves conflicts, and outputs a professional memo
explaining the tradeoff. Demo: 6,000 Commuter bikes, three suppliers, 
total cost $822K, on-time delivery, memo ready for approval."
